# Portfolio Copy

## Short Card Description

Built a synthetic credit risk monitoring workflow that transforms customer exposure, risk rating, payment, and default data into portfolio KPIs, high-risk watchlists, stress-test outputs, and BI-ready datasets.

## Long Project Description

Credit Risk Monitoring Dashboard is a synthetic credit portfolio analytics project designed to demonstrate how a financial services or credit insurance analytics team could monitor customer-level exposure, overdue balances, risk ratings, default indicators, and concentration risk.

The project builds a reproducible Python workflow that generates synthetic raw data, validates data quality, engineers risk features, calculates executive KPIs, identifies high-risk customers, applies stress-testing scenarios, and exports a clean Power BI-ready dataset. The SQL layer adds PostgreSQL-style portfolio monitoring queries for segmentation, default analysis, rating migration, concentration risk, watchlist logic, and dashboard export design.

The implementation is framed as a portfolio implementation and simulated business case. It does not use real company data or claim real business impact; it is designed to demonstrate practical credit risk analytics, KPI design, BI data modeling, and business communication.

## Skills List

Python, pandas, numpy, SQL, credit risk analytics, exposure monitoring, default analysis, watchlist logic, stress testing, KPI design, Power BI-ready modeling, data validation, pytest, CI/CD.

## Business Value Bullets

- Tracks credit exposure, overdue behavior, default indicators, and risk ratings.
- Identifies high-risk customers requiring review.
- Measures concentration risk by customer, country, industry, and rating.
- Produces clean BI-ready datasets for portfolio risk dashboarding.
- Demonstrates explainable stress testing and risk reporting logic.
